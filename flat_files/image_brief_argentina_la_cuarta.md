# Image Brief — Argentina "La Cuarta" Fan T-Shirt (SKU family: ARG-LACUARTA-BLK)

Photo set for the Amazon listing. 7 images: 1 main + 6 secondary. Amazon allows up to 9
per listing; the flat file carries the first 3 URLs (main, size chart, lifestyle) — add the
rest in Seller Central after the listing is created.

## Production method (read first)

**Never let an AI model re-render the print.** AI re-renders will distort the lettering,
star count, and year dates — and an inaccurate print image is a returns machine. Instead:

1. Generate or photograph the **blank black tee** scene (prompts below).
2. **Composite the actual print PNG** (the approved "ARGENTINA LA CUARTA" artwork file)
   onto the shirt in post (Photoshop/Canva smart-object mockup, or your POD provider's
   mockup generator). Warp/displace to follow fabric folds; reduce overlay opacity to
   ~95% and add a subtle fabric texture layer so it doesn't look pasted on.
3. Same artwork file every time — print position: centered chest, top of graphic
   ~7 cm below the collar seam, print width ~28 cm on size L (scale proportionally).

## Technical specs (all images)

- 3000 × 3000 px minimum (Amazon minimum is 1600 px longest side; 3000 enables full zoom), sRGB, JPEG quality 90+.
- Square 1:1 frame. Product fills ≥ 85% of the frame on the MAIN image.
- MAIN image background must be pure white **RGB 255/255/255** — no props, no text, no
  badges, no watermarks, no mannequin parts visible.
- Secondary images may use lifestyle backgrounds, text overlays, and infographics.

## IP guardrails (every image)

- No FIFA marks: no tournament emblem, mascot, trophy silhouette, "World Cup"/"FIFA"
  text anywhere — including blurred background signage.
- No AFA crest, no official Argentina kit (no replica jersey on models — plain clothing only).
- No player likenesses; generated models must not resemble real players.
- No stadium/brand logos, no broadcaster graphics, no beer/sponsor brands in scenes.
- Flags, sky-blue/white color schemes, and generic soccer balls are fine.

### 1. MAIN — ghost mannequin on white
- **Purpose:** search-results thumbnail; must read clearly at 100 px.
- **Base prompt:** "Professional apparel product photography, plain black unisex crew-neck
  t-shirt, invisible ghost mannequin effect, front view perfectly centered and symmetrical,
  natural drape with soft realistic wrinkles, pure white seamless background 255 255 255,
  even diffused studio lighting, no shadows on background, ultra sharp, 3:2 crop to square"
- **Composite:** overlay the La Cuarta print PNG, centered chest placement.
- **Check:** collar centered, hem straight, shirt fills ≥85% of frame, background pure white
  (verify with eyedropper at all 4 corners), zero text/props.

### 2. Size chart infographic
- **Purpose:** kill size-related returns (bullet 4 promises this image).
- **Layout:** flat tee silhouette with width/length arrows on the left; table S–3XL on the
  right with chest width and body length in **inches and cm** (pull numbers from your blank
  supplier's spec sheet — do not invent them); "Unisex fit — women size down for a fitted
  look" note at the bottom.
- **Style:** black/gold/sky-blue palette matching the design; white or light background;
  large legible type (min ~40 px at 3000 px wide).

### 3. Lifestyle — watch party (male model)
- **Purpose:** context + gift appeal; this is flat-file image slot 3.
- **Base prompt:** "Lifestyle photography, young man in his late 20s wearing a plain black
  crew-neck t-shirt, celebrating with arms raised on a living-room couch, watching soccer
  on an out-of-focus TV in the background, warm evening light, sky-blue and white paper
  streamers and a small generic Argentina flag on the wall, snacks on the coffee table,
  candid joyful expression, shallow depth of field, no logos anywhere, TV screen shows only
  a blurred green pitch with no graphics or broadcaster overlay"
- **Composite:** print PNG onto the shirt, matched to torso perspective/lighting.
- **Check:** TV must show no recognizable match footage, scoreboard, or channel bug.

### 4. Female model — street style
- **Purpose:** show unisex fit on a female body; supports "for men and women" bullet.
- **Base prompt:** "Street-style photography, young woman wearing a plain black crew-neck
  t-shirt in a relaxed unisex fit, casually knotted at the hip, light blue jeans, golden-hour
  sunlight, colorful Buenos Aires-style painted neighborhood street background softly out of
  focus, genuine smile, walking toward camera, no visible logos or signage text"
- **Composite:** print PNG onto the shirt.

### 5. Print close-up
- **Purpose:** show print quality and detail (ball texture, gold stars, lettering).
- **Method:** crop from the MAIN composite at 100% — or shoot macro of a real printed
  sample if you have one (preferred; also proves color accuracy).
- **Framing:** three-quarter crop centered on the ball + "LA CUARTA" lettering; slight
  fabric texture visible through the ink.
- **Optional overlay:** small caption "Vivid, durable print — machine washable".

### 6. Styled flat lay
- **Purpose:** matchday-outfit context; scroll-stopper variety.
- **Base prompt:** "Top-down flat lay photography, plain black t-shirt neatly laid on light
  wood floor, styled with blue jeans, white sneakers, a classic black-and-white soccer ball,
  and a small sky-blue and white striped scarf without any text or crest, soft daylight from
  the left, minimal composition with generous spacing, no logos, no lettering on any item"
- **Composite:** print PNG onto the shirt (flat, no warp).
- **Check:** the scarf must remain crest-free and text-free — plain stripes only.

### 7. Fabric & fit infographic
- **Purpose:** back the quality bullets; reduce "is it thick/soft?" questions.
- **Layout:** center: the MAIN shirt image. Around it, 4 callouts with simple line icons —
  "100% cotton midweight jersey" · "Double-stitched hem & sleeves" · "Classic unisex fit,
  S–3XL" · "Machine wash cold, inside out". Footer strip: "Original independent fan art —
  designed by Tstars".
- **Style:** same black/gold/sky-blue palette; keep total text minimal and large.
