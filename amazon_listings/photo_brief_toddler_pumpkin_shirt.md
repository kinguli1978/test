# Photo Brief — Toddler Jack-O'-Lantern Halloween T-Shirt (7 Images)

Product: bright orange toddler crew-neck tee, short sleeve, black smiling jack-o'-lantern face print (two triangle eyes, triangle nose, wide toothy grin). Sizes 2T–5T.

---

## How to use this brief with ChatGPT

1. **Always attach the real product photo** (the orange shirt image) to every ChatGPT request as a reference, and open each prompt with: *"Use the attached shirt as the exact product — same orange color, same print, same proportions. Do not redraw or restyle the pumpkin face."* AI loves to "improve" designs; a print that doesn't match the shipped product is an Amazon policy violation (misleading imagery) and a returns machine.
2. **Best practice for print accuracy:** let ChatGPT generate the scene/model/background, then composite your real design file onto the shirt in Photoshop/Canva afterward. Treat AI output as the scene, not the artwork.
3. Generate at the largest size available, then upscale. Every deliverable: **JPEG, sRGB, 1:1 square, minimum 1600×1600 px** (this activates Amazon zoom; 2000×2000 preferred), under 10 MB.
4. Keep the **same shirt color value in all 7 images** (pumpkin orange, roughly #F47B20). Color inconsistency between images is a top cause of "not as pictured" returns.
5. ~75% of traffic is mobile. Check every image at thumbnail size (~150 px): if the pumpkin face or the infographic text isn't legible there, regenerate with the subject larger.

### Amazon rules that apply to ALL seven images
- Square 1:1, ≥1600 px, JPEG, sRGB.
- No watermarks, no borders, no mockup artifacts (hanger tags, fold lines from templates).
- Nothing pictured that isn't included (no candy bucket "included", no second shirt unless labeled as sold separately).
- No Amazon logos/badges ("Best Seller", Prime, etc.), no review quotes, no price or promo text ("SALE", "50% off") anywhere.
- Kids' apparel note: **child models ARE allowed on secondary images** but never in suggestive poses; children must be fully clothed, natural settings.

## IMAGE 3 — SIZE CHART INFOGRAPHIC (top conversion image)

**Purpose:** sizing doubt is the #1 blocker and the #1 defect/return driver for toddler tees. This image does more for CVR than any lifestyle shot.

**Prep:** measure a real shirt per size first. Do not let AI invent numbers — give ChatGPT your actual measurements.

**ChatGPT prompt:**
> Clean e-commerce size chart infographic on a white background. Left third: the attached orange pumpkin-face toddler t-shirt shown flat with two thin measurement arrows labeled "Chest Width (A)" across the chest and "Length (B)" from shoulder to hem. Right two-thirds: a simple table with dark-grey grid lines, header row in orange (#F47B20), columns: Size | Chest Width (in) | Length (in) | Fits Age. Rows: 2T [X | X | 1–2 yrs], 3T [X | X | 2–3 yrs], 4T [X | X | 3–4 yrs], 5T [X | X | 4–5 yrs]. Bottom line in small text: "Measured flat. Between sizes? Size up for layering." Modern sans-serif font, large and legible at thumbnail size, no other decoration, 2000x2000 px.

**QC:** replace every X with real measurements; proofread the table — AI-generated text garbles easily, so it's often better to have ChatGPT make the layout and typeset the numbers yourself in Canva.
